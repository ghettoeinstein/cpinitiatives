About

Watts Wellness Collective delivers outpatient behavioral health, substance-use recovery groups, and trauma-informed counseling, with mobile outreach into underserved pockets of South Los Angeles.

Services

Health & Wellness

Population Served

Adults and youth needing behavioral health or recovery support

Eligibility

Open referral, sliding-scale fees, Medi-Cal accepted.

Referral Information

Clinical partners can submit a warm referral; self-referral by phone or walk-in.

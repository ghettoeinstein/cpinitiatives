Gardena Family Resource Network

Legal aid, food access, and family stabilization services.

✓ Verified CPI Partner Accepting Referrals

About

Gardena Family Resource Network connects families to legal aid clinics, food assistance, and financial-literacy coaching, with a focus on keeping families intact through reentry and economic hardship.

Services

Whole-Person Support

Population Served

Families, caregivers, justice-impacted parents

Eligibility

Families and individuals residing in South Bay / South LA service area.

Referral Information

Referrals accepted from any CPI partner or direct community request.

About CPI

One Community. Connected.

Strong organizations already exist. The missing layer is coordination.

Why CPI Exists

Fragmentation creates barriers. People navigating reentry, housing instability, or behavioral health crises are too often asked to find their own way between organizations that don't talk to each other. CPI exists to make the community's existing capabilities visible, trusted, navigable, and collaborative.

Community Partners Initiatives is founded on the conviction that no single organization can solve systemic recidivism or institutional vulnerability in isolation. By establishing a shared standard of unity, transparency, and resource convergence at cpinitiatives.org, we align local institutions into a unified support network—ensuring that every person returning to our community meets an open door, a stable home, and an accessible path to self-determination.

Our Model

Discover→ Connect→ Coordinate→ Measure→ Improve

Founding Thesis

The community already contains extraordinary organizations. CPI exists to make them easier to find, trust, and work with.

Operating Principle

A referral is not an outcome. A connection is complete only when another organization accepts the handoff.

Community works better when we work together.

Whether you lead an organization, fund community work, or want to volunteer — there's a place for you in the CPI network.

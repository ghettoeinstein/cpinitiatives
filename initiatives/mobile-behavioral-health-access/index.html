Mobile Behavioral Health Access

Bringing behavioral health and recovery support directly into underserved neighborhoods.

Problem

Many residents in need of behavioral health or recovery support cannot reach a clinic due to transportation barriers, work schedules, or trust gaps with formal institutions.

Goal

Launch a mobile behavioral health unit serving 6 neighborhoods on a weekly rotation.

Current Partners

  • Watts Wellness Collective

What We Need

  • Clinical partners
  • Community outreach partners
  • Transportation
  • Funding

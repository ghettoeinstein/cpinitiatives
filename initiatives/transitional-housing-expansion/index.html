Transitional Housing Expansion

Expanding transitional and supportive housing capacity across South Los Angeles.

Problem

Demand for transitional beds in South Los Angeles regularly outpaces supply, leaving people returning from incarceration or homelessness without a stable place to land in their first critical weeks.

Goal

Add 40 new transitional and supportive housing placements within 12 months.

Current Partners

  • Community Hope Center
  • Gardena Family Resource Network

What We Need

  • Housing providers
  • Property partners
  • Funders
  • Transportation support

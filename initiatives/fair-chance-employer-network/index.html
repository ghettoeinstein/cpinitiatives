Forming Workforce & Economic Mobility

Fair-Chance Employer Network

Building a coalition of employers committed to fair-chance hiring across the South Bay.

Problem

Job seekers with conviction histories face systemic barriers to employment even after completing training, and employers willing to hire fair-chance often work in isolation without shared pipelines or mentorship support.

Goal

Recruit 15 committed fair-chance employers and place 100 job seekers in year one.

Current Partners

  • Second Chance Works
  • Horizon Workforce Alliance

What We Need

  • Employers
  • Job training partners
  • Mentors
  • Recruiters

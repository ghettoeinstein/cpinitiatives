What We Do

Four Pillars of Community Support

Every partner in the CPI network operates within one or more of these shared areas of support.

🏠

Housing Stability

Housing navigation, transitional housing, supportive housing, permanent housing.
💼

Workforce & Economic Mobility

Training, credentialing, employment, entrepreneurship, fair-chance opportunities.
🩺

Health & Wellness

Behavioral health, recovery, healthcare, trauma-informed care.
🤝

Whole-Person Support

Legal services, transportation, food access, financial support, family services.
